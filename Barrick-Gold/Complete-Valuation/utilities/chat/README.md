# Research decisions and handoff

Sanitized decisions from the research workflow. Full chat transcripts, personal
workstation paths, credentials and provider-controlled observations are excluded.

- One shared infrastructure: `data`, `outputs`, `src`, `utilities`.
- Full edition: `outputs/report_unificato`; concise edition: `outputs/report_paper`.
- Shared report figures: `outputs/img/publication`; graph: `utilities/graphify`.
- Physical commodity forecasts and reconciled FCFF are required for corporate integration.
- Option pricing under Q and physical prediction under P remain distinct tests.
- OOS warm guesses use a successful strictly earlier origin; current information
  is used to refit, and initialization source/hash is recorded.
- Existing numerical freezes remain traceable; new methods do not silently replace them.
- Publication labels are removed only when supported by the evidence.

See [the shared scientific plan](../docs/PHYSICAL-FORECAST-PLAN.md) and
[output index](../../outputs/README.md). Operational checkpoints and original
project archives remain outside the public repository.
