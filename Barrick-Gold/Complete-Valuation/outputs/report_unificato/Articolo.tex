\newcommand{\abstracttext}{%
% The generated results are input locally here because the title page is set
% before Chapter 00 defines them globally.
\begingroup\input{chapters/generated/results_20261024}%
This study builds and audits a stochastic valuation architecture for
Barrick Mining Corporation within Starting Finance Club PoliTo Research. Eight
teams contribute probability, econometrics, Monte Carlo, historical gold
dynamics, stochastic volatility and jumps, option calibration, mine-level
operating forecasts and discounted cash flow; unification assigns each variable
to one source authority and links the layers through explicit date, unit,
measure and accounting contracts. The central question is how much the choice
of gold-price model matters for a discounted operating-margin proxy of Barrick
when production, costs and discount-rate shocks are held fixed.
Black--Scholes/GBM, Heston, Bates--Poisson and Full Bates--Hawkes are
calibrated, with nested refinements, to 64 GLD calls of 2 September 2026
selected by a Chebyshev design from 605 eligible quotes, with the trust's
0.40\% sponsor fee treated as carry. Each richer model fits at least as well as
the model it contains (IV RMSE \CalBSIVRMSE, \CalHIVRMSE, \CalBIVRMSE{} and
\CalWIVRMSE~bp); calibration fit does not establish predictive or economic
superiority. The engines drive daily gold paths from the XAUUSD spot of
USD~4,391.32/oz at the valuation time on a dated clock: Barrick's Q1--Q2 2026
actuals are excluded and only flows after 2 September 2026, to 31 December
2030, are valued. Across 8,192 paths the median proxy lies between
USD~\ValMedianLow{} and \ValMedianHigh~bn for all four models, a spread no
larger than the numerical sensitivity of a single model
(USD~\NumericalMedianLow--\NumericalMedianHigh~bn across path counts and
seeds); the terminal value carries about \TerminalShare\% of the mean. In
\OosDates{} equal-search rolling repricing tests Full Bates--Hawkes has the
lowest error, \OosWRMSE~bp, but no model beats the persistence of the
implied-volatility surface. Three producing copper mines add about
USD~\CuDeltaLow--\CuDeltaHigh~bn of explicit flows to 2030. The price laws are
risk-neutral: no GLD-to-gold basis or $\mathbb Q$-to-$\mathbb P$ mapping is
validated, and mine-level operating uncertainty is not propagated. The proxy
has no equity bridge, so no per-share value or market comparison is reported.
The distributions are controlled research sensitivities, not physical
forecasts, confidence intervals, fair values, targets or investment
advice.\endgroup}
