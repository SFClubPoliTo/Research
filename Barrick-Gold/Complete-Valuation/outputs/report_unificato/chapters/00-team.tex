\chapter*{Team}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Team}
\markboth{Team}{Team}

\noindent
This report is the integrated work of the Research area of Starting Finance
Club PoliTo. Each of the eight teams wrote one of Articles 1--8, published
between April and September 2026; the final review combined them into this
report and the companion Paper. Team~8 supplies the option-implied gold-price
dynamics, Team~4 the production and unit-cost layer, Team~5 the cash-flow and
discounting identities and Team~3 the simulation diagnostics; Teams~1, 2, 6
and 7 provide the probability, econometric, stochastic-process and
historical-volatility foundations.

\section*{Individual contributions (70+ project hours each)}

\noindent
Each researcher below dedicated 70+ hours to the project and is a core author
of the article of their team. Each researcher was also responsible for teaching
the content of their article to the rest of the Research team and took part in
an on-campus event at the Politecnico di Torino, presenting to the Financial
Education team and to the club's active and associate members, in order to
build a shared knowledge base and ensure high-quality training for all.
