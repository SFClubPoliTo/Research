\markboth{Team}{Team}

\noindent
This report is the result of a full year of work by the Research area of
Starting Finance Club PoliTo. Seventeen students of engineering, mathematics,
data science and quantitative finance from the Politecnico di Torino and the
Universities of Turin and Padua, organized in eight teams, started from the
foundations---measure theory, time series, Monte Carlo---and built, piece by
piece, a complete stochastic valuation of a listed mining company. Each team
wrote and published one of Articles 1--8 between April and September 2026;
the following months were spent reviewing each other's work, rerunning the
code on dated market data, correcting, harmonizing the notation and joining
eight independent studies into one coherent framework.

\smallskip\noindent
Behind these pages are more than 1,300 hours of study, coding, calibration and
review, carried out alongside university courses and exams. We are proud of
what this effort produced, and of the shared knowledge it leaves to the club.

\medskip
\noindent{\large\bfseries Individual contributions (70+ project hours each)}\par\smallskip
\noindent
Each researcher below dedicated 70+ hours to the project as a core author of
their team's article, taught its content to the rest of the Research team and
presented it at an on-campus event at the Politecnico di Torino to the
Financial Education team and the club's active and associate members.
