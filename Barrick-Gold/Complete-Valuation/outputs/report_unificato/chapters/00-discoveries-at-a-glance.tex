\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}
\markboth{Abstract}{Abstract}

\noindent
This study builds and audits a stochastic valuation architecture for Barrick
Mining Corporation. Eight teams of the Starting Finance Club PoliTo Research
area contribute probability, econometrics, Monte Carlo, historical gold
dynamics, stochastic volatility and jumps, option calibration, mine-level
operating forecasts and discounted cash flow; unification assigns each variable
to one source and links the layers through explicit date, unit, measure and
accounting contracts. The central question is how much the choice of
gold-price model matters for a discounted operating-margin proxy of Barrick
when production, costs and discount-rate shocks are held fixed.
Black--Scholes/GBM, Heston, Bates--Poisson and Bates--Hawkes are calibrated,
with nested refinements, to 64 GLD calls of 2 September 2026 (605 eligible
quotes, sponsor fee of 0.40\% as carry; IV RMSE \CalBSIVRMSE, \CalHIVRMSE,
\CalBIVRMSE{} and \CalWIVRMSE~bp). They drive daily gold paths from the XAUUSD
spot of USD~4,391.32/oz on a dated clock: only flows after 2 September 2026
are valued. Three producing copper mines add about
USD~\CuDeltaLow--\CuDeltaHigh~bn of explicit flows to 2030. Three findings
follow.

\begin{tcolorbox}[
  title={Discovery 1: the gold-price model matters less than numerical noise},
  colback=sflightblue, colframe=sfblue, boxrule=0.7pt, arc=1mm,
  left=3mm, right=3mm, top=1mm, bottom=1mm
]
\small
Across 8,192 paths the four gold-price laws give median operating proxies
between \textbf{USD~\ValMedianLow{} and \ValMedianHigh~billion}. Changing only
the number of paths or the seed moves a single model's median across
\textbf{USD~\NumericalMedianLow--\NumericalMedianHigh~billion}, so the medians
do not rank the models (Figure~\ref{fig:discoveries-multimodel}).
\end{tcolorbox}

\begin{tcolorbox}[
  title={Discovery 2: the terminal value carries the result},
  colback=sflightblue, colframe=sfblue, boxrule=0.7pt, arc=1mm,
  left=3mm, right=3mm, top=1mm, bottom=1mm
]
\small
Median flows to 31 December 2030 range from \textbf{USD~\ValExplicitLow{} to
\ValExplicitHigh~billion}; the perpetuity after 2030 carries about
\textbf{\TerminalShare\%} of the mean proxy. The terminal assumptions, not the
gold model, decide the level.
\end{tcolorbox}

\begin{tcolorbox}[
  title={Discovery 3: no option model beats the persistence of the IV surface},
  colback=sflightblue, colframe=sfblue, boxrule=0.7pt, arc=1mm,
  left=3mm, right=3mm, top=1mm, bottom=1mm
]
\small
In \OosDates{} rolling repricing tests Bates--Hawkes has the lowest error of
the four models (\OosWRMSE~bp), but carrying the last observed
implied-volatility surface forward remains marginally more accurate
($R^2 = \OosWRPersistence\%$, DM $p = \OosWDMPersistenceP$).
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{-0.3cm}
\noindent{\small\textbf{What these numbers are.} The price laws are
risk-neutral: no GLD-to-gold basis or $\mathbb Q$-to-$\mathbb P$ mapping is
validated, and mine-level operating uncertainty is not propagated. The results
are operating-margin proxies without an equity bridge
(Section~\ref{sec:company-financial-perimeter}), hence no per-share value or
market comparison: controlled research sensitivities, not physical forecasts,
fair values, target prices or investment advice.}
